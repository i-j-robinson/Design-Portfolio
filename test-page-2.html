WiFi AR Visualizer Concept

UX/UI, AR, Animation
At Xfinity, we are always looking for innovative ways to create the best in-home WiFi experience for our customers. Currently, I am leading the design of the WiFi Visualizer concept, addressing customer pain points with solutions that lean heavily into Augmented Reality. This case study highlights the design process, excerpts of a presentation I gave to our SVP of Xfinity, and our goals for the future of this product.

My Role

Lead UX/UI Designer

Client

Xfinity

Timeline

Ongoing

Team

Isaiah Robinson (Me)

The Challenge

At Xfinity, we can use AR to measure the strength of our IOT device's WiFi connections. How can we take advantage of this technology to curate meaningful experiences for our customers in a way that provides real-world value?

Our Goals

Research and discovery on the value of AR in today's marketplace, investigate the value of AR for our customers, and work with our engineering partners to deliver this experience.

Understanding AR in todays world

I started this project by understanding how AR is used in today's market, how customers use AR in their daily lives, and the value it provides on the business end.
