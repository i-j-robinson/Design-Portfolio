Video Storytelling:
Terra Digital App V1

iOS App
Terra is a platform specifically tailored to reduce friction and complexity in the video storytelling process. We provide premium storyboard templates that allow users to plug in their own content to create agency quality videos.

UX Design
UI Design

Client

Terra Digital

The Challenge

Video storytelling is complicated. Companies, Nonprofits, and content creators are using video content to better serve their audiences, but creating high quality video content is difficult to do without a budget or knowledge of video editing.

Our Goals

Our team aimed to design an app that provides premium storyboard templates that allow users to plug in their own content to create agency quality videos.

How The World Makes Videos

When I joined the Terra team, we started with having conversations with people who currently make or are interested in making video content.
We were able to draw key insights why video content is important in todays world, and how we can make it easier to create videos. .
Users believe there is no easy way to create high-quality video content.
54% of consumers want to see more video content
Videos are a consumers' favorite type of content on social media
We also looked into indirect and direct competitors of Terra, hoping to draw inspiration and find opportunities for improvement.

 What we loved about Squarespace is how easy it is to create a webpage with an aesthetic that you like. SS is not as robust as a WordPress or Webflow but is built for those who need a website, have little knowledge of the web, and little budget for a web developer. We see Terra in the same light - we want to give users the power of storytelling through video, even if they may not be video editors or know the video space.

Another app we liked was the Unfold mobile app on iOS. Unlike the Adobe Premier and iMovie mobile apps, Unfold utilizes simple UI elements to make video editing easy for the user. It gives the user complex film editing templates like film, minimal, and paper for different aesthetics. We felt that Terra could benefit from simple UI elements like those in the Unfold app that complement the different storyboard templates we wanted to create.

We iterated on multiple user flows that highlight how one would utilize a Terra storyboard video template.

A Solution for Video Storytellers

Our final design for Terra V1 gives the user a new way to tell their own story through video content.
We start here, where the user has the ability to edit a storyboard template. When a template is selected, the user must give their new project a name, and can now edit the storyboard template with the appropriate content. Each template has individual blocks that are to be personalized by the user based on the content they have and the story they want to tell.
The Key Facts content block serves as a place where content creators can utilize text blocks in their story. On tap, the user is able to input whatever copy is needed in this block of content. These blocks allow the user to easily edit their clips, input their own content, and add music within a storyboard that fits their desired needs.
Once finished, the user can download their video directly to their phones camera roll, or share their video to a friend through a message. Users now have a high quality piece of content to post on their social channels to grow and engage their audiences.
