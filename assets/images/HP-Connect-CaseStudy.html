The Challenge

With the workforces transitioning to remote enviornments, IT departments need modern solutions for managing their machines in remote enviornments. HP Connect seeks to bring a new solution to BIOS Management, by creating an experience that allows for IT admins to manage the BIOS of their organizations devices through a cloud service.

Our Solution

I developed a user experience that simplifies all aspects of BIOS (i.e. Policy Creation, Secret Management, Device Management) through enhanced user flows and a visual identity within HP's design language that resonates with IT professionals.

The What & Why of HP Connect for MEM

HP Connect for Microsoft Endpoint Manager is for IT managers who need to remotely configure, secure, and update the BIOS of HP commercial PCs. It allows managers to create policies for groups of devices customizing over 100 different HP BIOS settings.


BIOS are the firmware that act as a middleman between the OS and the hardware. BIOS identify, configure test and connect computer hardware to the OS immediately after a computer is turned on.Crucial to all devices, BIOS firmware have many functions, including making sure your device will run without issue by connecting the OS to the Firmware and testing componets like your hard drive, graphics cards and keyboard are all working correctly.


In discussions with ITDMs for customers such as 3M and Federal Reserve Bank the topic has increasingly turned to how to enable manageability in a “cloud first” world. These customers, and many others, are looking for ways to free their workflows from their corporate network

Creating Workflows & Workshopping HP Connect

Due to the complexity of HP Connect for those not familiar with computer BIOS, I was able to utilize workshops with stakeholders within the HP Connect team, including Product Marketing, IT and Project Management. Working within am agile spint enviornment, we would begin each sprint with a workshop to talk through individual userflows of the experience & also develop low-fidelity wireframes.

Developing the HP Connect Design Library

While designing HP Connect, I was able to utilize the HP PSSW design system Venner to create a custom HP Connect Design Library, utilizing the Nielsen 10 heuristics for Interface Design.
